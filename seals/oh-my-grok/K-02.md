**Title:** Vendored MCP runtimes ship without their upstream licenses

**Body:**

`scripts/build-mcp-runtimes.sh` vendors `ast-grep-mcp` and `lsp-tools-mcp` from code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent into `vendor/`, and the repository ships only their `dist/` builds under the repository's MIT `LICENSE`.

Upstream:
- oh-my-openagent's `LICENSE.md` (since 2025-12-24) puts its own code under the Sustainable Use License 1.0, which limits use to internal, non-commercial or personal purposes, allows distribution only free of charge, and asks that licensing notices are not removed. Its `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` lists `lsp-tools-mcp` as MIT (pi-lsp-client, Yeongyu Kim) with its own `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`, and does not list `ast-grep-mcp` as a third-party component, so `ast-grep-mcp` appears to fall under the Sustainable Use License.

In this repository, `vendor/superpowers/` carries its MIT `LICENSE`, and the Go dependencies under `vendor/github.com/` carry theirs, but `vendor/ast-grep-mcp/` and `vendor/lsp-tools-mcp/` carry none.

Suggested fix: copy `LICENSE.md` (and the relevant notice) into `vendor/ast-grep-mcp/`, copy `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` into `vendor/lsp-tools-mcp/`, and say in the README that the `ast_grep` MCP server is under the Sustainable Use License, not MIT. If that license does not fit the project, an ast-grep MCP server under a permissive license could replace it.
