**Title:** docs(readme): say which sites the skills contact

**Body:**

README.md line 210 promises: "The skills make HTTP requests to your target site only. No data leaves your machine."

The `geo-brand-mentions` skill does more than that, by design. `skills/geo-brand-mentions/SKILL.md` tells the agent to use WebFetch and web search for the brand name on YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, Quora and Stack Overflow (lines 68 to 130), to run a Python snippet that sends the brand name to the Wikipedia and Wikidata search APIs (lines 83 to 109), and to fetch a Wikipedia article for the founder's name (line 113). `skills/geo/scripts/brand_scanner.py` makes the same Wikipedia and Wikidata calls (lines 121 and 137).

The behavior is what a brand scan needs. The sentence is what is wrong, and a user who relies on it for a client under NDA sends the client's name to third parties without knowing.

Change: replace line 210 with

> The skills request your target site: its pages, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt` and sitemap. `geo-brand-mentions` also searches YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn, Quora, Stack Overflow, Wikipedia and Wikidata for the brand and founder names you give it. Leave that skill out of an audit if those names must not leave your machine.
